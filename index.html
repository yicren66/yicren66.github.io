Biography 个人简介

Sep 2023 – Jul 2027

I am a senior undergraduate student in the College of Information Engineering at Northwest A&F University (Project 985 & 211), pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science and Technology (GPA: 3.3/4.0, 83.07/100, Rank: top 19.35%; Grade A in Probability & Statistics, A- in Advanced Mathematics, Linear Algebra, C Programming, and Operating Systems).

My research focuses on lightweight and transferable computer vision, 3D point cloud perception, few-sample learning, and end-to-end intelligent systems across smart agriculture and industrial visual inspection. I have authored papers as first author in Agriculture (JCR Q1, under review) and Proc. SPIE (MVDL 2026), co-invented a national patent, and led two national software copyrights.Previously, I also worked as a Data Development Intern at Taibao Technology (China Pacific Insurance Group) in Shanghai, focusing on financial LLM benchmarks and domain fine-tuning.

我是西北农林科技大学（国家 985 / 211 工程高校）信息工程学院计算机科学与技术专业 2023 级本科生（GPA：3.3/4.0，加权平均分 83.07，专业排名前 19.35%；概率论与数理统计 A，高等数学、线性代数、C 语言程序设计、操作系统均为 A-）。
